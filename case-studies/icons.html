Soft icons

The tree icons from this site, large. Soft, one base colour and its shades: a raised face, a dark shadow to the lower right, a light one to the upper left, engraved details. The folders are glass: blue to magenta behind a frosted panel. SVG, so they scale. Click one to open the file.

Files: assets/icons/*.svg. Rebuild with python3 tools/build-icons.py. The 3D folder above is tools/glass-folder.py (STYLE = 'soft') rendered in Blender. The earlier glass version is kept in versions/glass/.
